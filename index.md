## About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at UIUC, advised by Prof. [Fan Lai](https://www.fanlai.me/). I'm broadly interested in Machine Learning Systems and efficient ML. Recently, I've become increasingly interested in **Physical AI and Robotics**, especially the systems that make training and deploying embodied models efficient.

Previously, I was a software engineer at ByteDance, working on ML training systems and inference on TPUs.

Before that, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/), where I was also advised by [Fan Lai](https://www.fanlai.me/), focusing on Machine Learning Systems.

I earned dual bachelor's degrees from the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/) and [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). At Michigan, I had the privilege of working with [Mosharaf Chowdhury](https://www.mosharaf.com/) and [Amber Liu](https://websites.umich.edu/~amberljc/) on Large Language Model (LLM) inference.
