  • Bioinformatics

    Genome-wide detection of somatic mosaicism at short tandem repeats

    Sehgal A., et al.

    Bioinformatics, 2024

    In brief

    Post-zygotic mutations at Short Tandem Repeats create genetically distinct subsets of cells within an individual, giving rise to somatic mosaicism. No reliable statistical method existed for detecting these mutations at genome-wide scale. We introduce prancSTR, which models sequencing reads as a mixture to estimate mosaic fractions without a matched control, and use it on 1000 Genomes data to reveal population-specific patterns of mosaic mutation.

  • Genome Biology

    LongTR: genome-wide profiling of genetic variation at tandem repeats from long reads

    Ziaei Jam H., et al.

    Genome Biology, 2024

    In brief

    Long reads can span tandem repeats that short reads can’t. LongTR genotypes tandem repeats genome-wide from long-read sequencing and reports full allele sequences, not just lengths, opening long and complex repeats to population-scale study.

    Fecal microbiota transplantation efficacy for Clostridium difficile infection: a trans-kingdom battle between donor and recipient gut microbiomes

    Kazemian N., Ramezankhani M., Sehgal A., et al.

    Scientific Reports, 2020

    In brief

    Why does fecal microbiota transplantation clear recurrent C. difficile infection in some patients but not others? Studying bacteria and fungi together in donors and recipients, this work uses random forests and k-means clustering to connect gut microbiome features with treatment outcome.

  • IEEE TVT

    Contract theory based medium access contention resolution in TDMA cognitive radio networks

    Gupta N., Dhurandher S. K., Sehgal A.

    IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 2019

    In brief

    In cognitive radio networks, unlicensed users compete for time slots on shared spectrum. This paper uses contract theory to design incentives that resolve that contention in TDMA-based networks, so the channel is used more efficiently.

  • IEEE Syst. J.

    A contract theory approach-based scheme to encourage secondary users for cooperative sensing in cognitive radio networks

    Dhurandher S. K., et al.

    IEEE Systems Journal, 2019

    In brief

    Cooperative spectrum sensing works best when many users take part, but sensing costs each of them energy. This paper proposes a contract-theory incentive scheme that rewards users for cooperating, improving how reliably free spectrum is found.
