**#WIALT (What I Am Listening to)** — Chemist + Druggist's *Pharmacy in Person*

![Pharmacy in Person episode artwork](/assets/img/wialt/2026-03-jane-lambert-neurodivergence-pharmacy-in-person.png)

Having experienced the AuDHD pathways as a parent to an amazing daughter, this conversation resonates with me.

In this episode of the Chemist + Druggist "Pharmacy in Person" series, Jane Lambert - founder and CEO of Makewell Clinic and ECG Healthcare Group and 2025 Women Leader Award Winner for Healthcare - shares a compelling and personal account of her career journey.

As a nurse turned healthcare entrepreneur and adoptive parent, Jane brings lived experience to a conversation that matters enormously for community pharmacy. She explores the challenges faced by neurodivergent individuals, the critical role pharmacists can play in identifying and supporting those with ADHD and autism, and why empathy must sit at the heart of every patient interaction.

Jane reflects honestly on building accessible healthcare services and the need for clinical teams to develop a much deeper understanding of neurodivergence - a topic that is increasingly relevant to every pharmacy counter in the UK.

A thoughtful, grounding listen for anyone working in or around community pharmacy and primary care.
